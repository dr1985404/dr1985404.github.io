Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how MCCQE1 Prep Platform collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personally identifiable information. We may collect anonymized data including:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page (via Google Analytics)

  • Ad interaction data (via Google AdSense)

2. Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to serve advertisements. Google may use cookies to show ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site. For more information, visit Google's advertising policy.

3. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this platform. Google Analytics collects anonymized usage data. No personal information is shared with us. You may opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

4. Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We use cookies to:

  • Remember your quiz preferences and progress
  • Enable Google AdSense ad personalization
  • Support Google Analytics usage tracking

You can control cookies through your browser settings at any time.

5. Third-Party Links

Our site may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

6. Children's Privacy

This platform is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Continued use of the platform constitutes acceptance of any changes.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please use our Contact page.
