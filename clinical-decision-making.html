Clinical Decision-Making (CDM) – MCCQE1 Scenarios

The Clinical Decision-Making (CDM) component of the MCCQE1 assesses your ability to make critical decisions in a clinical setting. It focuses on the most important steps in diagnosis and management rather than just factual recall.

🧠 The CDM Strategy for MCCQE1

  • Identify the 'Next Best Step': Always ask what needs to be done *right now* to stabilize the patient or confirm the diagnosis.
  • Avoid Redundancy: In CDM write-in questions, do not list three tests if one is the gold standard.
  • Safety First: Prioritize ABCs (Airway, Breathing, Circulation) in all emergency scenarios.
  • Cost-Effectiveness: Choose the least invasive, most effective test first (e.g., Ultrasound before CT in a child).
  • Ethical Alignment: Ensure your decisions respect Canadian medical ethics and legal standards.
💡 High-Yield Pearl: In CDM cases involving chest pain, the 12-lead ECG is almost always the first diagnostic step and should be performed within 10 minutes of arrival.
