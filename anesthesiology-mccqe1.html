Anesthesiology – MCCQE1 High-Yield Notes

Key Anesthesiology concepts tested in the MCCQE1 exam, organized for International Medical Graduates (IMGs) preparing for Canadian medical licensure.

🔑 Key Topics in Anesthesiology for MCCQE1

  • Pre-operative assessment and risk stratification (ASA classification)
  • Airway management: RSI, difficult airway algorithm, Mallampati score
  • General vs. regional anesthesia: indications and contraindications
  • Common anesthetic agents: propofol, ketamine, succinylcholine, rocuronium
  • Malignant hyperthermia: trigger agents, clinical features, treatment with Dantrolene
  • Post-operative nausea and vomiting (PONV): risk factors and management
  • Local anesthetic toxicity: LAST syndrome, treatment with Intralipid 20%
  • Spinal vs. epidural anesthesia: differences, indications, complications
  • Pain management: WHO analgesic ladder, opioid dosing, patient-controlled analgesia
  • Monitoring standards: ECG, SpO2, ETCO2, temperature, blood pressure

⚠️ High-Yield Clinical Pearls

  • Malignant Hyperthermia: Triggered by succinylcholine and volatile agents → treat with IV Dantrolene immediately
  • Can't intubate, can't oxygenate (CICO): Emergency surgical airway (cricothyrotomy)
  • Succinylcholine contraindications: Hyperkalemia, burns >48h, crush injury, denervation
  • Spinal headache (PDPH): Positional, treat with blood patch if persistent
  • Ketamine: Dissociative anesthetic, maintains airway reflexes, good for hemodynamically unstable patients
