Frequently Asked Questions about MCCQE1 Prep

What is the MCCQE1 exam?

The MCCQE1 (Medical Council of Canada Qualifying Examination Part I) is a computer-based exam required for all physicians seeking licensure in Canada. It assesses clinical decision-making, medical knowledge, and patient management across multiple specialties. International Medical Graduates (IMGs) must pass it to practice medicine in Canada.
