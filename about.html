Our Mission

This platform was created to support International Medical Graduates (IMGs) preparing for the Medical Council of Canada Qualifying Examination Part 1 (MCCQE1). We believe that financial limitations should never be a barrier to a medical career in Canada.

All resources — including the question bank, clinical notes, and specialty reviews — are provided completely free of charge and are sustained through ethical, non-intrusive advertising.

What We Offer

The platform covers all major MCCQE1 domains including Internal Medicine, Surgery, Pediatrics, OB/GYN, Psychiatry, Preventive Medicine, Emergency Medicine, Anesthesiology, and Ethics. Content is regularly updated to reflect current MCC objectives and exam patterns.

Content Quality

All materials are curated by medical professionals with expertise in Canadian licensing examinations. Questions are reviewed for clinical accuracy and alignment with the MCC's Medical Expert and CanMEDS frameworks.
